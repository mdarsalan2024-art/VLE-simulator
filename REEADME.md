VLE Simulation is an interactive tool for exploring vapor–liquid equilibrium in binary mixtures. At a specified pressure and composition, it calculates the system’s bubble-point/dew-point temperatures and plots T-x-y diagram. The calculation uses Antoines equation,Roults law and azeotropic mixtures handling and assumes constant pressure and ideal mixture conditions.
